CrossPoster

One video, posted everywhere at once.

CrossPoster is a personal Mac app for publishing one short video to several social networks at once: YouTube, Instagram, Facebook, Threads, TikTok and Bluesky. It writes the caption once, adjusts it for each network, and shows the views, likes and followers of the connected accounts in one place.

CrossPoster is used only by its developer, with accounts the developer owns or manages. It isn't available for download, and nobody else can sign in to it.

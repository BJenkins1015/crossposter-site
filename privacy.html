CrossPoster

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

CrossPoster is a personal Mac app that publishes videos to social media accounts and shows those accounts' statistics. It is used only by its developer, and only with accounts the developer owns or manages. It isn't offered to the public. This policy explains what CrossPoster accesses and what it does with it.

What CrossPoster accesses

When an account on YouTube, Instagram, Facebook, Threads, TikTok or Bluesky is connected, CrossPoster may access:

  • basic profile information for that account, such as its name, username and profile picture;
  • the videos, captions and settings published through CrossPoster;
  • statistics for the account and its posts, such as followers, views, likes, comments and shares.

CrossPoster doesn't access private messages, contacts, or accounts other than the ones connected to it.

How it's used

Only to publish videos to the connected accounts and to show their statistics inside the app. The data isn't used for advertising, profiling, or anything else.

Where it's stored

  • Everything stays on the developer's own Mac. Sign-in tokens are kept in the macOS Keychain, and statistics and post history are kept in the app's local database.
  • CrossPoster has no servers of its own and doesn't send this data anywhere except back to the platform it came from.
  • One exception: Threads can only fetch a video from a web link, so a video posted to Threads is uploaded briefly to a private cloud storage location under an unguessable link and deleted as soon as Threads has it.

Sharing

CrossPoster doesn't sell, rent or share data with anyone. It uses no analytics, ads or tracking.

YouTube

CrossPoster uses YouTube API Services. By connecting a YouTube account you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's use of data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy. You can remove CrossPoster's access at any time in your Google Account security settings.

Removing access and deleting data

  • Disconnecting an account in CrossPoster deletes its sign-in tokens and stored statistics from the Mac.
  • Access can also be removed from each platform: Google Account → Security → Third-party connections; Instagram and Facebook → Settings → Apps and websites; Threads → Settings → Account → Website permissions; TikTok → Settings → Security → Manage app permissions; Bluesky → Settings → Privacy and security → App passwords.
  • To ask for any data CrossPoster holds about an account to be deleted, email benjenks@gmail.com. It will be deleted within 30 days.

Children

CrossPoster isn't meant for children and doesn't knowingly collect data from anyone under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
